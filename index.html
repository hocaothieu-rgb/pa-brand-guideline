Brand identity · 2026

Peter Ahn. Sales & leadership, in deep green and paper.

Peter Ahn is CCO at TigerBeetle and has built sales teams at Google, Dropbox and Slack. His channel and talks teach the long game of sales. The identity had to feel like him: direct, built on trust, and serious without being corporate.

We kept the PA monogram he already had, fixed the letter that read like an "R", gave it a wide name in Panell Wide, and built the whole system from deep green, paper and one shape: a staircase that climbs one step at a time.

Client
Peter Ahn
Scope
Logo, motion, social, video, print, merch
Studio

01 · From Peter Ahn Sales to PA

The old wordmark draws itself into the new mark.

The green underline of the old logo becomes the stroke that traces the monogram, then the name settles beside it. Every rule below comes from that mark.

02 · Channels

YouTube, LinkedIn and the avatar share one frame.

The staircase carries three facts about Peter, one per step. The dot field sits on the edge that the avatar or the platform UI covers, so nothing important is lost on any screen. The YouTube banner is the one black-and-white exception; LinkedIn and everything else run green.

03 · Video system

Thumbnails: one face or two, always three lines.

Every long-form thumbnail uses the same green halftone background, the new lockup in the top-left corner and a three-line METAFORA title with the middle line boxed. Interviews open the layout to two people without changing anything else.

03 · Shorts editing guide

Shorts get one accent: mint on deep green.

Six pages for editors: the system, colour and type, the text card, text on b-roll, safe zones for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, and the mistakes to check before export.

04 · Print

Posters, cards and paper carry the same staircase.

Deep green uncoated stock for anything Peter hands over, paper for anything people write on. The mark is debossed, never foiled.

05 · Merchandise

A black tee that reads from across the room.

On the back, the mark is broken into a halftone that fades from white to grey, with the two words Peter teaches on either side. On the front, a three-line note and a small grey mark.
